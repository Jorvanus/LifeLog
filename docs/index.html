Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

LifeLog is a personal timeline app. It records where you go and what you're doing, entirely for your own use. This policy explains exactly what LifeLog collects, why, and — most importantly — what happens to it.

The short version

Everything LifeLog records stays on your iPhone. There is no LifeLog server. Nothing is uploaded, synced, sold, or shared with advertisers or analytics companies. LifeLog does not track you across other apps or websites.

What LifeLog collects, and why

  • Location. LifeLog uses your location (including in the background) to detect when you arrive at and leave places, so it can build your timeline of visits automatically.
  • Motion & Fitness data. Used to recognize walking, running, cycling, and travel between places.
  • Apple Health data (with your permission) — sleep, workouts, heart rate, breathing rate, steps, distance, and related activity signals. LifeLog reads this data to fill in and enrich your timeline; it never writes to Apple Health.

Where it's stored

All of the above is stored in a local database inside the LifeLog app on your device, protected by iOS's own file encryption. LifeLog has no backend, no account system, and no cloud sync — there is nowhere else for this data to go.

The one place LifeLog talks to a server

To turn a raw location into a place name ("Home", a café, an office), LifeLog asks Apple Maps to look up nearby places for a coordinate you visited. That lookup is handled by Apple's MapKit framework under Apple's own Maps & privacy terms — LifeLog's developer never sees or receives this data. No other network requests are made by LifeLog.

Backups

You can create a local backup file from within LifeLog at any time. That file is created on your device and stays there unless you choose to save or send it somewhere — for example, using iOS's own Share Sheet to save it to Files, iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself. LifeLog never uploads a backup automatically.

Your control over your data

Because everything lives only on your device, you are always in complete control of it. You can erase all recorded data from LifeLog's Settings at any time. Deleting the app deletes everything LifeLog ever recorded, since there is no server copy to separately remove.

Tracking & advertising

LifeLog contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children's privacy

LifeLog is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If LifeLog's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
